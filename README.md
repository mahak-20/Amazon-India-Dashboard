# Amazon India Sales & Operations Dashboard

An executive business intelligence dashboard designed to provide a clear, non-technical overview of Amazon India's retail performance[cite: 6]. Built for management review, this solution consolidates sales, profitability, product performance, order fulfillment, payment methods, and regional metrics into a single, intuitive visual interface[cite: 6].

---

## Project Context & Objective

Developed as a **Sapphire IQ** project, the goal of this dashboard is to translate raw e-commerce data into simple charts, graphs, and KPIs[cite: 6]. Designed specifically for a non-technical stakeholder (Ravi), the dashboard allows leadership to monitor trends, evaluate operational efficiency, and make data-driven decisions without needing technical expertise or manual spreadsheet analysis[cite: 6].

---

## Business Objectives & Dashboard Breakdown

The dashboard is organized around four core analytical pillars[cite: 7, 8, 9, 10]:

### 1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance[cite: 7]
* **Business Question:** *How is the business performing overall?*[cite: 7]
* **Key Metrics & Visuals:**
  * **Headline KPIs:** Total Sales[cite: 7], Total Profit[cite: 7], Total Orders[cite: 7], Total Units Sold[cite: 7], Average Order Value (AOV)[cite: 7], and Profit Margin %[cite: 7].
  * **Trend Analysis:** Monthly Sales & Profit Trend[cite: 7].
* **Expected Insight:** Enables quick tracking of whether overall revenue and profitability are growing or declining over time[cite: 7].

### 2. Category & Product Performance[cite: 8]
* **Business Question:** *Which products and categories are driving the business?*[cite: 8]
* **Key Metrics & Visuals:**
  * Category-wise Sales[cite: 8], Profit[cite: 8], and Units Sold[cite: 8].
  * Top 10 Products by Sales[cite: 8].
* **Expected Insight:** Highlights top-performing categories and hero products while identifying underperforming items that require attention[cite: 8].

### 3. Order Status & Revenue Loss[cite: 9]
* **Business Question:** *How many orders are successfully completed and how many are being lost?*[cite: 9]
* **Key Metrics & Visuals:**
  * **Order Volume Breakdown:** Delivered[cite: 9], Shipped[cite: 9], Returned[cite: 9], and Cancelled Orders[cite: 9].
  * **Loss Metrics:** Return Rate %[cite: 9], Cancellation Rate %[cite: 9], and Category-wise Returns & Cancellations[cite: 9].
* **Expected Insight:** Quantifies operational leakage and shows how returns and cancellations impact overall business performance[cite: 9].

### 4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance[cite: 10]
* **Business Question:** *Which payment methods, fulfillment methods, and states are generating business?*[cite: 10]
* **Key Metrics & Visuals:**
  * **Payment Channels:** Sales and Profit by Payment Method[cite: 10].
  * **Fulfillment Channels:** Sales and Profit by Fulfillment Method[cite: 10].
  * **Regional Breakdown:** State-wise Sales[cite: 10], Profit[cite: 10], and Orders[cite: 10].
* **Expected Insight:** Identifies key geographic markets and evaluates how different payment and fulfillment options contribute to revenue and margins[cite: 10].
